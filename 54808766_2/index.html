Our Story

Slime's Braai & Mleqwa Delicious Food is a proudly local street food business based in Belhar, Cape Town. Our business was created with one simple goal: to provide delicious, affordable, and freshly prepared traditional African meals to students, workers, families, and visitors in the community. Belhar is home to many students who live away from their families while studying at nearby colleges and universities. Many of them miss the taste of home-cooked meals, and we wanted to create a place where they could enjoy comforting food at reasonable prices. Our menu is inspired by traditional South African recipes that have been passed down through generations. Every meal is prepared with care to provide customers with authentic flavours that remind them of home.

Food Preparation

At Slime's Braai & Mleqwa Delicious Food, we believe that great food begins with fresh ingredients and careful preparation. Every morning we select quality meat, fresh vegetables, spices, and other ingredients from trusted local suppliers to ensure that every meal meets our high standards. Our menu includes popular South African favourites such as braaied steak, grilled chicken, wors, pap, chakalaka, samp and beans, mogodu, fried fish and chips, and traditional Mleqwa chicken.

Why You Should Choose Us

Choosing Slime's Braai & Mleqwa Delicious Food means choosing quality, value, and authentic South African flavours prepared with care. We understand that our customers want delicious meals that are affordable, filling, and freshly prepared, especially students and working people with busy schedules.
