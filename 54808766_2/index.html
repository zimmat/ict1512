Our Story

Slime's Braai & Mleqwa Delicious Food is a proudly local street food business based in Belhar, Cape Town. Our business was created with one simple goal: to provide delicious, affordable, and freshly prepared traditional African meals to students, workers, families, and visitors in the community. Belhar is home to many students who live away from their families while studying at nearby colleges and universities. Many of them miss the taste of home-cooked meals, and we wanted to create a place where they could enjoy comforting food at reasonable prices. Our menu is inspired by traditional South African recipes that have been passed down through generations. Every meal is prepared with care to provide customers with authentic flavours that remind them of home. Over the years, we have become more than just a food stall. We have built strong relationships with our customers by providing friendly service, generous portions, and consistent quality. Many of our customers visit us several times a week because they know they will always receive fresh food and a warm welcome. We believe that food has the power to bring people together, create lasting memories, and strengthen communities. Our dream is to continue growing while staying true to our values of honesty, quality, affordability, and excellent customer service.

Food Preparation

At Slime's Braai & Mleqwa Delicious Food, we believe that great food begins with fresh ingredients and careful preparation. Every morning we select quality meat, fresh vegetables, spices, and other ingredients from trusted local suppliers to ensure that every meal meets our high standards. Our menu includes popular South African favourites such as braaied steak, grilled chicken, wors, pap, chakalaka, samp and beans, mogodu, fried fish and chips, and traditional Mleqwa chicken. Each meal is prepared using traditional cooking methods that preserve authentic flavours while ensuring excellent quality. Our meat is carefully seasoned with a special blend of herbs and spices before being cooked over an open flame to create the rich smoky flavour that our customers enjoy. Side dishes are cooked fresh throughout the day to maintain their taste and quality. We also pay close attention to hygiene and food safety by keeping our cooking area clean, storing ingredients correctly, and following proper food handling practices. Every meal is prepared with care because we believe our customers deserve the very best. Our generous portions, affordable prices, and freshly prepared meals have made us one of the favourite street food vendors in Belhar. We work hard every day to ensure that every customer leaves satisfied and eager to return.
